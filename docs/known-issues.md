## Workforce sample data loader (2026-10-15) - DEV ONLY
- `apps/worker/scripts/sample-workforce-year.ts` REPLACES all workforce data of an existing organisation with one simulated year (join date -> today): holidays, attendance (weekly offs/holidays/leave days/absences by the real day-close job), leave requests + approval steps + balance ledger, Earned Leave (real engine), HR corrections, 45-day goals (+milestones/updates), work tasks (+events, linked to goals), announcements, salary structures. Then `node dist/seed/sample-payroll-run.js` (apps/api, env `PAYROLL_FROM`/`PAYROLL_TO`) creates the payroll runs through the real service (Finance prepares, Founder approves, Finance records the payout; the last month stays CALCULATED).
- To remove append-only history it disables the guard triggers of 5 tables inside one transaction and verifies they are enabled before commit. Local hosts only, never production. Texts end with `[sample data]`; salaries and birthdays are fictional. Audit-log rows from earlier loads remain (append-only) and refer to deleted ids.
- Reconciliation run on 2026-10-15 (SQL, independent of the engines): payslip working/paid/loss-of-pay days vs attendance and leave (160 payslips, 0 mismatches), gross vs structure x paid days (138 full-month payslips, 0 mismatches; the other 22 are prorated joiners and were not recomputed), leave ledger chain and final balances (0 breaks), debits vs approved leave days (0), Earned Leave credits vs eligible days / 20 (24 users, 0 mismatches), goal progress vs current/target (0), attendance gaps (0).
